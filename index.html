Our Legacy

Nearly a Decade of Redefining Barber Culture

Founded by entrepreneur Bruno John Shirima, THE INC Barber Shop was established to bridge the gap between high-end city-center salons and local outer-suburban barbers in Nyamagana District. Over our 6–9 year journey, we have pioneered our signature "executive-lite" model, combining elite standards with local accessibility.

We don't believe in standard service. Every customer is treated as an executive. From our strict ultraviolet tool sanitization to our uninterrupted backup power loop, we ensure absolute comfort and complete safety.
